LESSON 02

How Do Search Engines Work?

Search engines help people find useful information across billions of web pages. They use automated systems to discover content, understand it, and present relevant results.

1. Crawling

Search engine crawlers discover URLs and retrieve accessible page content. They can discover pages through links and sitemaps.

Example: Googlebot discovers a link to your website and requests that page.

2. Indexing

Search engines analyze the content they discover and may store information about it in their index. Not every crawled page is guaranteed to be indexed.

Example: A search engine analyzes your page title, text, images, and other available information.

3. Serving Search Results

When someone searches, the search engine selects and orders results it considers relevant to the query. Many factors influence which pages appear and where they appear.

Example: Someone searches for "petrol bunk management software". A useful, relevant page may be considered for those search results, but ranking is never guaranteed.

Important SEO Tip

Make important pages accessible, provide useful content, use descriptive page titles, and create clear internal links. These practices help search engines discover and understand your website.
