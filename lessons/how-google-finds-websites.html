LESSON 04

How Does Google Find Websites?

Google needs to discover web pages before it can crawl and potentially index them. It uses several methods to find URLs across the web.

1. Discovering Pages Through Links

Search engines can discover new pages by following links from pages they already know about.

Example: Your homepage links to your products page. Google may follow that link to discover the products page.

2. Discovering Pages Through XML Sitemaps

An XML sitemap is a file that lists important URLs on your website. It can help search engines discover pages.

A sitemap commonly uses a filename such as sitemap.xml.

Important: Submitting a sitemap does not guarantee that Google will crawl or index every listed URL.

3. What Is Googlebot?

Googlebot is the name commonly used for Google's web crawling software. It requests pages so Google can discover and process their content.

Example: Googlebot requests a public article on your website and retrieves its accessible HTML content.

4. Can Google Find Every Page?

No. Some pages may be difficult to discover because they have no links pointing to them, are unavailable, or have technical problems.

Important pages should have useful internal links and should be accessible to search engines when you want them to be discovered.

5. Does Discovery Guarantee Indexing?

No. Discovering a URL is different from crawling it, and crawling is different from indexing it.

  • Discovery: Google learns that a URL exists.
  • Crawling: Googlebot requests and retrieves a page.
  • Indexing: Google processes the page and may include it in its index.

Real-Time Example

Imagine your petrol bunk software website has a new page about inventory management. You link to that page from your homepage and include its URL in your sitemap. These steps can help Google discover the page, but crawling and indexing are not guaranteed.

Key Takeaway

Use clear internal links, maintain an accurate XML sitemap, and ensure important pages are accessible. These practices help search engines discover your website's content.
