LESSON 03

What Are the Different Types of SEO?

SEO has different areas that work together to improve a website's visibility in organic search results. Understanding these areas helps you identify what a website needs to improve.

1. On-Page SEO

On-page SEO means improving the content and HTML elements of individual web pages.

Examples:

  • Writing descriptive page titles.
  • Creating useful meta descriptions.
  • Organizing content with proper headings.
  • Adding relevant internal links.
  • Writing helpful, original content.

Real-time example: If you have a petrol bunk software website, create a useful page explaining your billing, inventory, and sales reporting features.

2. Off-Page SEO

Off-page SEO involves activities outside your website that can help build its reputation and authority.

Examples:

  • Getting relevant backlinks from trustworthy websites.
  • Publishing useful content that others want to reference.
  • Building genuine industry relationships.

Real-time example: An industry website references your software guide and links to your website. That link can help people discover your content.

3. Technical SEO

Technical SEO focuses on making your website accessible, understandable, and technically sound for search engines.

Examples:

  • Improving page loading performance.
  • Creating an XML sitemap.
  • Configuring robots.txt correctly.
  • Using HTTPS.
  • Fixing broken links and unwanted 404 errors.
  • Making pages mobile-friendly.

Real-time example: If an important page returns a 404 error, users and search engines may not be able to access the intended content.

4. Local SEO

Local SEO helps businesses improve their visibility for searches related to a particular location.

Examples:

  • Maintaining an accurate Google Business Profile.
  • Keeping business name, address, and phone details consistent.
  • Collecting genuine customer reviews.
  • Creating useful location-specific website pages.

Real-time example: A person searches for "software companies near me". Accurate business information can help a relevant local business appear in search results.

How Do These SEO Types Work Together?

Imagine launching a new business website:

  • On-page SEO: Explain your products clearly.
  • Off-page SEO: Earn relevant, genuine backlinks.
  • Technical SEO: Make important pages accessible.
  • Local SEO: Help nearby customers discover your business.

Key Takeaway

These SEO areas complement one another. Start with useful content and a technically accessible website, then improve your site's reputation and local visibility where relevant. No SEO technique guarantees a particular ranking.
